# LifeLink Account Testing

## Demo account
Email: `demo@lifelink.test`
Password: `LifeLink123`

The demo account is linked to a sample O+ donor profile in Demo Mode.

## New account test
1. Click Register.
2. Enter name, email, password and confirm password.
3. Click Create account.
4. You should see **Account created successfully**.
5. LifeLink automatically moves to Login.
6. Enter the same email and password.
7. Successful login opens the Home page.
8. The top-right user chip shows the user's name/email.
9. Click the user chip to view account details.
10. Open Dashboard to create, edit, view or delete the saved donor profile.

## Contact test
Go to Find Donors and click a sample donor's **Contact** button to reveal the phone number, or **Email** to reveal the email address.
